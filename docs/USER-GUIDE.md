## Statement process (detail)

1. Open **Financial Reporting**
2. Select **company** and **period**
3. **Trial Balance** — upload / review / download
4. **Journal Entries** — review adjustments
5. **Income Statement** — generate, review, export
6. **Balance Sheet** — review, export
7. **Cash Flow** — complete, export

Every step supports **Upload · Review · Download**.

## Recommended demo path (section 14 in the app)

1. Sign in as staff  
2. Sample Library → download Acme pack  
3. Documents → upload  
4. Statements → full TB→CF path  
5. Bank Rec → match  
6. Month-End Close → checklist  
7. New PBC Request  
8. Contact Center → short video  
9. Portal as `client@acme.com` → upload response  

---

## Client path

1. Sign in at `/` with client account  
2. Land on `/portal`  
3. **Documents** — upload bank statements, invoices, payroll  
4. **Requests** — see PBC items; mark uploaded  
5. Staff review in firm Documents / PBC screens  

---

## Safety note

Run **File Sanitizer** before sending files into AI tools. Prefer firm-approved AI only.
